## Native model bring-up

### Edge0-145B

[PR #174](https://github.com/jshahbazi/stark/pull/174) was closed without merge. Its head preserves attempts 01–23, including source and license intake, native readers, F32 reference and Metal execution, worker MTP, paging and resident-memory pressure, local 8K loading, direct routes and wider prefill. Main carries the [original plan](../EDGE0_PLAN.md).

On the pinned Q3K checkpoint on M5 Max, the 128-input, 16-output MTP-off screen measured **14.70 → 54.18 prefill tok/s** and **11.98 → 14.78 decode tok/s** from grouped default to the 32-token schedule across two candidate builds. Each cell used six requests. At 1,024 input tokens, the direct and 32-token schedules took **55.0 s and 20.1 s** respectively. Comparison against each schedule's F32 reference was bit-exact on the sealed research builds. The newer schedules lacked long-context, paging, cancellation, MTP and peak-memory qualification. No Edge0 prefix reuse was available on this branch. ### Inkling 2 Mini

The hosted Inkling branch contains the original fixed-three-pass checkpoint's native Metal path, packed prefill, source-aligned precision, vision, DSpark and prefix restoration. On the 128 GiB M5 Max, with 4,096 input tokens, context 4,352, 256 outputs, vision and DSpark resident, and drafting off, the source-aligned worker won all 16 matched pairs. Median prefill reached **683.34 tok/s greedy and 685.31 tok/s sampled**, roughly **1.78×** the prior worker, and request time fell about 23%. Useful decode moved **33.26 → 31.38 tok/s greedy** and **32.09 → 30.31 tok/s sampled**. The precision change can alter text from the prior Stark worker. The final source-shaped oracle checks matched 16 F32 vectors across four stock-schedule inputs. A separate 2,048-token restore with a changed 471-token suffix cut prefill **4.139 → 0.891 s** and matched all 24 output IDs. Two-image embedding controls and short serial DSpark verification passed their bounded checks. The DSpark verifier added wall time in its diagnostic.
