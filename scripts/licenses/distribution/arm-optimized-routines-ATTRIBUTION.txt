The pinned PyTorch ARM NEON header identifies its single-precision vector
exponential implementation as copied from Arm Optimized Routines:

https://github.com/ARM-software/optimized-routines/blob/c154f238b01fd62b84172680126d70dd919b221a/math/aarch64/advsimd/expf.c

The source adaptation is the vector range-reduction and polynomial schedule;
PyTorch's pinned header adjusts special-case bounds and handles its finite
GELU input range. The cited upstream file carries this copyright:
